⚙️ Official Municipal Grievance Lifecycle

From Citizen Grievance to On-Ground Resolution

How road damage and drinking water leaks get reported, assigned by department leaders, and repaired by crews.

01 CITIZEN STAGE

👤 1. Citizen Reports Grievance

Citizens observe road damage (potholes) or water pipeline leakage in their colony, attach an on-site photo, pin GPS coordinates, and submit. An official tracking ticket (e.g. CF-2026-1013) is generated.

02 LEADER STAGE

🏛️ 2. Department Leader Assigns

The Department Leader / Chief Inspector reviews incoming tickets, verifies severity, and assigns the work order to the specialized on-ground crew lead (e.g. Water Board Lead K. Suresh or Roads Lead Ramesh Kumar) with an SLA deadline.

03 WORKER STAGE

🛠️ 3. Worker Executes & Resolves

The on-ground crew mobilizes to the site, seals the burst pipe / patches the asphalt, uploads verified completion photo proof, and signs off. Citizen receives resolution notification with Before vs After proof!
